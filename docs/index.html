Never leave someone on read again.

Onread is an Android app that watches for texts from the people you choose and reminds you to reply, then cancels the reminder once you do.

Download the app View the code

The problem

You read a message, think "I'll answer later," and then life happens. Hours or days go by, and the person you care about is still waiting. Onread helps you keep up with the conversations that matter most.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your people

    Choose the contacts you want Onread to keep an eye on.

  2. 2

    Get a reminder

    When they text you, Onread reminds you to reply at the time you set.

  3. 3

    Reply and relax

    As soon as you answer, Onread notices and cancels the reminder for you.

Features

  • Tracked contacts

    Only the people you choose trigger reminders. No noise from everyone else.

  • Custom reminder times

    Decide when you want to be reminded to reply.

  • Automatic reply detection

    Reply normally, and the reminder disappears on its own.

  • Works with your texts

    Built for regular SMS and RCS messages in Samsung Messages.

Wireframes

How we built it

Onread is a native Android app written in Kotlin in Android Studio. It stores your tracked contacts and reminders in a local Room database, listens for new messages, and schedules reminders with Android's alarm system.

  • Kotlin
  • Android Studio
  • Room database
  • GitHub

Your privacy

Onread works on your phone. Your messages and contacts stay on your device.
